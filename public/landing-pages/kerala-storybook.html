About KathaVerse

KathaVerse is a living archive of Kerala's cultural heritage — a place where ancient stories breathe again. We preserve the folklore, mythology, legends, ritual traditions, and sacred art of Kerala through beautifully illustrated, immersive storytelling. From the mysteries of the Yakshi to the grandeur of King Mahabali, from the living ritual of Theyyam to the genius of Perunthachan, each story is told with cultural care and authenticity. This is a community effort — every contribution helps keep these stories alive for generations to come.
